# Tutorial 7 project files (SEES0082 PEIB)

Material for Tutorial 7 (reproducible coding with R, Stata, GitHub and AI agents).

| Folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| `tutorial7_code/` | The partly pre-coded project that students download as `tutorial7_code.zip`: task lines replaced by `your_code_here (Question x.y)`; `fdi_generate.R/.do` left out on purpose (Question 2.1). |
| `tutorial7_code_complete/` | The complete project (`tutorial7_code_complete.zip`), released after the tutorial; every answer is marked `# Response to Question x.y`. |
| `tutorial7_code_lecturer/` | The complete project with the lecturer's paths in Part 1 of `main.R` / `main.do` (code here, data and output in the module's OneDrive folder `week7/project/tutorial7_project`); it runs from this folder. |
| `tutorial7_project/data/` | The two raw data files (`tutorial7_data.zip`) at the paths the main scripts expect. Intermediate and final data and the outputs are not stored here; the scripts re-create them. |

## Data sources
- `tutorial7_project/data/fdi/raw/cm-fdi-projects.csv`: Bandara, P. and the Net Zero Industrial
  Policy Lab. Cleantech Manufacturing FDI Dataset, edition 2026.10.05. Johns Hopkins University.
  https://cmfdi.netzeropolicylab.com. Licence: CC BY 4.0.
- `tutorial7_project/data/sol/raw/installed-solar-pv-capacity.csv`: IRENA (2026) - processed by Our
  World in Data. Installed solar PV capacity.
  https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/installed-solar-pv-capacity. Our World in Data publishes its
  data under CC BY 4.0; third-party data remain subject to the terms of the original provider
  (IRENA, Renewable Energy Statistics 2026).

## How these folders are made
They are generated by `make_project7.py` (course folder, `Claude outputs/Week7/Tutorial/scripts`).
Edits made here are not overwritten silently: they are picked up into the generator in the next
revision round, and the folders are then generated again.
